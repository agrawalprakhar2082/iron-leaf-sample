Our story

Who we are

Ironleaf Engineering Works is a second-generation foundry and machine shop in Howrah, producing cast iron and ductile iron components for pump, valve and railway manufacturers since 1987.

Our Jalan Complex unit combines moulding, induction melting, fettling and CNC machining under one roof, so customers receive finished, inspected parts from a single supplier. We regularly supply buyers across India and export to the Middle East, Europe and East Africa.
